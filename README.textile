h3. Blues Background Formula

Not an arrangement -- a one-page reference card for harmonizing improvised
background parts over a 12-bar blues, as taught on a whiteboard in D-flat.
One person invents a background rhythm on the tonic, everyone repeats it, and
from then on each player picks any note out of the formula and moves it with
the chords.

The whole page (four transpositions plus the 12-bar grid) is generated from a
single pitch at the top of the file, so @concertTonic = d'@ can be changed to
reprint the entire sheet in any key.
